# Template: a finished monthly letter

This shows the shape and tone only. Everything in {braces} is filled from the report and the founder's answers. The real letter never contains braces.

---

Subject: {Company} | {Month Year} investor update

Dear investors and friends,

{Month} in one line: {best news}, {second-best news}, and {what is coming}.

**Highlights**

- {Headline KPI}: {value} ({+x%} vs {last month}), about {value x 12} annualized.
- {Second KPI}: {value} ({+x%}), a record.
- Profitable: about {value} after all operating expenses. (Or: burn {value}, runway {n} months.)
- {Number} new customers live, including {a notable first}.

**Treasury**

- Cash at {date}: {value}. Runway: {n} months.
- Operating expenses: {value}, including {value} one-time. Recurring about {value} a month.

**Traction** (vs {last month})

- {KPI 1}: {value} ({+/-x%})
- {KPI 2}: {value} ({+/-x%})
- {KPI 3}: {value} ({+/-x%}), {one clause on why, if it fell, and when it recovers}

**Product and go-to-market**

{Two or three sentences: what shipped, what it unlocks, what ships next and when. New customers by name, linked, if they agreed to be named.}

**Team**

{Who joined, in one sentence.}

**Up next: {next month}**

- **{News since month-end}.** {One or two sentences, with a number if there is one.}

**Fundraising**

{Only if the founder asked: "We plan to raise {round} in {timing} to {purpose}."}

**Honest reflection**

{What is not working, in one or two sentences. Then the fix and when.}

**How you can help**

1. Intros to {exact type of customer}.
2. Intros to {exact type of investor or hire}.

{Standing close line.}

{Founder names}
